## The honest headline

Two separate clocks, and only one of them can be sped up with money:

1. **The marriage — FAST.** California has *no waiting period*. A license is valid the
   moment it's issued and good for 90 days. LA County offers same-day "express" civil
   ceremonies by appointment. You can realistically be legally married the day after you
   land, or even the same day, if we pre-book the appointment. ✅

2. **The green card — NOT fast, and money can't change that.** There is **no premium
   processing** for a family/marriage-based green card (Forms I-130 + I-485). The
   realistic timeline is **~12–20 months** to the actual green card no matter who you
   hire. What paying *does* buy you:
   - **Speed and accuracy of preparation** (attorney or service files a clean package in
     days, not weeks, and avoids delays from mistakes/RFEs).
   - **A work permit (EAD) in ~3–5 months** and a **travel document (Advance Parole)**,
     so Rohan can keep working and can travel internationally while the case is pending.

So the plan optimizes the two things that *are* in our control: **get married
immediately**, and **file a flawless package the moment you're eligible.**

## ⚠️ Two things that could derail everything — read before you book any travel

- **Do NOT travel internationally again after filing the I-485 until you have Advance
  Parole or the green card** — leaving the U.S. with a pending adjustment is treated as
  *abandoning* the application. (Narrow exception: if Rohan holds **H-1B or L-1** status,
  those allow travel on the visa itself even with a pending I-485. We need to confirm his
  status — see Open Questions.) This is why the plan is: finish Europe → land July 14 →
  *then* marry and file → stay put until the EAD/Advance Parole combo card arrives.
- **This whole "concurrent filing" path assumes Rohan is currently in the U.S. in valid
  status and last entered legally (with inspection).** If so, he can "adjust status"
  without leaving. If not, it becomes consular processing (a different, slower path). See
  Open Questions — this is the single most important fact I need from you.
