# Document Checklist

Gather these. Most can be collected before you even land. Scan everything to PDF.

## A. Proof the marriage is real and legal

- [ ] **Certified copy of the marriage certificate** (after the July wedding).
- [ ] **Bona fide marriage evidence** — the more, the better. Officers look for a shared
      life:
  - [ ] Joint lease or mortgage / proof you live together at the Marina del Rey address
  - [ ] Joint bank account statements; joint credit cards
  - [ ] Each other named as beneficiary on insurance / 401k
  - [ ] Joint utility bills, phone plan, streaming, etc.
  - [ ] Photos together over time (trips, holidays, with families) — incl. the Europe trip
  - [ ] Travel itineraries / boarding passes together (Europe trip is great evidence)
  - [ ] Affidavits/letters from friends & family attesting to the relationship
  - [ ] Any joint property, pets, shared trips, correspondence

## B. Meg (U.S. citizen petitioner / sponsor)

- [ ] **Proof of U.S. citizenship** — U.S. passport (photo page) **or** birth certificate
      **or** naturalization certificate.
- [ ] **For I-864 Affidavit of Support:**
  - [ ] Most recent **federal tax return** (transcript preferred) — last 3 years ideal
  - [ ] W-2s / 1099s for the most recent year
  - [ ] Recent pay stubs (last 6 months) and/or employer letter
  - [ ] Income must be **≥ 125% of the federal poverty guideline** for your household
        size (household of 2 ≈ low-$25,000s in 2026; verify current HHS guideline). If
        Meg's income is below the line, options: count assets (savings/investments at 3×
        the shortfall), or add a **joint sponsor** (also files an I-864).
  - [ ] If Meg didn't file taxes for a year, a written explanation / exemption note.

## C. Rohan (beneficiary / applicant)

- [ ] **Passport** — photo/bio page; valid.
- [ ] **Most recent I-94** (download from cbp.gov/I94) — proves lawful entry & status.
- [ ] **Visa stamp** and any status documents:
  - [ ] If H-1B/L-1/O-1: approval notice **I-797**
  - [ ] If F-1/OPT: **I-20** and current EAD
  - [ ] Any prior USCIS notices / current EAD / advance parole
- [ ] **Birth certificate** with certified English translation if not in English.
- [ ] **2 passport-style photos** (also need for the forms).
- [ ] If ever in removal proceedings, arrested, or visa denials — documentation (discuss
      with attorney).

## D. Medical (Form I-693)

- [ ] Schedule with a **USCIS-designated civil surgeon** (find via uscis.gov/tools or
      ask the attorney; draft email in `07-email-drafts.md`).
- [ ] Bring vaccination records and passport ID.
- [ ] Doctor returns the **I-693 in a sealed envelope** — **do not open it**; USCIS
      rejects an opened envelope. Valid 2 years.

## E. Prior marriages (either spouse, if applicable)

- [ ] Divorce decree(s), death certificate(s), or annulment(s) for every prior marriage —
      for **both** Meg and Rohan.

## F. Photos & logistics

- [ ] 2 passport photos each (white background, 2"x2"), for I-485 / I-765 / I-131.
- [ ] A way to make a **full copy** of the filed package before mailing.
- [ ] USPS/FedEx tracking when you mail.
