## 2. LA County Clerk — phone script to book license + express ceremony

Call **800-201-8999** (Beverly Hills branch / RR/CC), or use marriage.lavote.net online.

> "Hi — I'd like to book a **marriage license appointment** and an **express civil
> ceremony** at the **Beverly Hills branch** for **Wednesday, July 15** (or Thursday,
> July 16). We'd like a **confidential license** (we live together). What appointment
> times are available, and can the license and ceremony be done the same visit? What ID
> should we bring, and how do we order certified copies the same day?"

Have ready: both names, the Marina del Rey address, a card for the $91 + $25 fees.
