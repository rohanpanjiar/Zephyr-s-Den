# Forms & Fees (2026)

## The forms in your concurrent package

| Form | Name | Who completes it | Filing fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I-130** | Petition for Alien Relative | Meg (petitioner) | **$675** paper / $625 online |
| **I-130A** | Supplemental Info for Spouse Beneficiary | Rohan | $0 (filed with I-130) |
| **I-485** | Application to Register Permanent Residence | Rohan | **$1,440** (includes biometrics) |
| **I-864** | Affidavit of Support | Meg (sponsor) | $0 |
| **I-765** | Application for Employment Authorization (EAD) | Rohan | **$260** (with pending I-485) |
| **I-131** | Application for Travel Document (Advance Parole) | Rohan | **$630** |
| **I-693** | Report of Medical Exam & Vaccination Record | Civil surgeon | (you pay the doctor, ~$200–500) |

### Totals

- **Minimum (I-130 + I-485 only):** ~**$2,115**
- **Full package (+ I-765 work permit + I-131 travel doc):** ~**$3,005**
- Plus the medical exam paid to the doctor (~$200–500) and certified marriage
  certificate copies (~$17 each).

> **Note on the $235 USCIS Immigrant Fee:** that fee applies to **consular processing**
> (immigrant visa abroad), **not** to adjustment of status inside the U.S. For your
> concurrent AOS filing you do **not** pay it.

## Payment rules — important, gets packages rejected

- **Separate payment per form.** Do **not** combine into one check. One check (or money
  order) for the I-130, a separate one for the I-485, another for the I-765, another for
  the I-131. A single combined payment can get the **entire package rejected**.
- Pay by **check or money order** payable to **"U.S. Department of Homeland Security"**
  (write it out in full — no abbreviations). Credit card is possible via **Form G-1450**,
  one per form.
- If filing online isn't used (I-485 generally isn't fully online and can't be filed
  online concurrently with I-130), file the whole package **by mail** to the correct
  **USCIS lockbox** for California AOS filings. The exact lockbox address is on the I-485
  page at uscis.gov — confirm at filing time, as addresses change.

## Where to get the forms

All free from **uscis.gov/forms** (latest editions only — USCIS rejects outdated form
editions). The current edition date is printed at the bottom of each form; download fresh
the week you file.

## Filing checklist (order of assembly)

1. Cover letter listing everything enclosed.
2. Each form, signed in ink (Meg signs I-130 & I-864; Rohan signs I-130A, I-485, I-765,
   I-131).
3. Separate payment clipped to each form.
4. Supporting documents behind each form (see `05-document-checklist.md`).
5. 2 passport photos each for I-485, I-765, I-131 (label lightly in pencil on the back).
6. Keep a **complete copy** of the entire package before mailing; send with tracking.
