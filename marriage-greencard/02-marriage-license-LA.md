# Getting Married in LA County — the Fast Part

This is the part that can happen within a day of landing. California has **no waiting
period**: a marriage license is valid immediately and good for **90 days**.

## ⏰ Booking window: 3 weeks out

**LA County opens appointments only up to 3 weeks in advance.** For a **July 15** wedding,
the slot becomes bookable around **June 24**. Book that morning — Westside express slots
fill fast. See `00-BOOK-NOW.md` for the exact play.

Payment: cash, check, credit/debit card, or money order.

## Two license types — pick one

- **Confidential license** — *recommended for you.* No witness required at the ceremony.
  Requirement: the couple must be living together before the wedding (you are). Not part
  of the public record; only the two of you can request copies.
- **Public license** — requires **at least one witness** at the ceremony. The office can
  provide a witness for $20 if you don't bring one.

## What to bring (both of you)

- Valid government photo ID (passport or driver's license). Passport is ideal for Rohan.
- If either of you was previously married: date the prior marriage ended; bring the
  divorce decree / death certificate / annulment (some offices ask, the form requires the
  details — see worksheet).
- No blood test, no proof of residency, no citizenship requirement to get married. Your
  immigration status does **not** affect your right to marry.

## How to book (do this now, before you land)

LA County uses an online system (**marriage.lavote.net**) to reserve appointments. Steps:

1. **Now:** Start the **online marriage license application** at marriage.lavote.net. Fill
   it out in advance — it generates a **confirmation/tracking number**. Save it.
2. **~June 24 (3 weeks before July 15):** Return and click **"Make an Appointment" →
   "Marriage License and Express Ceremony"** at the **LAX Courthouse** branch for
   **Wed July 15** (Beverly Hills as backup).
3. Complete the required waiver; save the appointment confirmation.

> You can't reserve July 15 until ~June 24 (the 3-week window). If online slots are full
> that morning, call **800-201-8999** to check all seven branches — phone script in
> `07-email-drafts.md`.

## On the day

1. Arrive ~10 min early with IDs (and witness, if you chose a public license).
2. Complete/confirm the license at the counter, pay $91.
3. Proceed to the express ceremony, pay $25. Officiant performs the ceremony.
4. **You're married.** Order **certified copies** of the certificate before you leave (or
   note how/when to request them). USCIS needs a certified copy.

## After the wedding

- The officiant returns the signed license to the county; the marriage is then registered
  and certified copies become available (usually ~2 weeks; ask about expedited/same-day
  copy service when you're there).
- Keep at least one certified copy for the USCIS green card package; scan it.
